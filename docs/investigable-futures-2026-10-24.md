# Investigable futures

This pass keeps the chapter layout and native scrolling while adding:

- An eight-forecast highlights route, chapter takeaways, saved reading position (local device only), and searchable/filterable overview. Links to omitted forecasts reopen the complete book.
- Quantum evidence checklist, gene-delivery route comparison and a transparent annual fusion-energy model. The model is explicitly hypothetical and never adjusts the forecast odds. Gene stages now change from DNA to cell delivery to patient outcomes to access.
- Unverified future milestones labelled as requirements, and unscored scenarios de-emphasized instead of displayed as headline percentages.
- Consistent dark theme, larger metadata, keyboard year control and outside reference curves in the evidence dashboard. Dashboard code is separately loaded.
- Forecast detail panels expose existing probability reasoning, domain-level counterarguments, signals, source/publisher coverage and limitations. Publisher counts are not independence counts. These panels do not invent quantitative base rates or claim all sources are independent.
- PQC display aligned with its existing December 2030 deadline. The compute indicator now distinguishes top-five global-compute evidence from the top-two frontier-compute hinge criterion.
- Deployment uses the successfully published commit marker, not checkout HEAD. Failed builds retry; the old site remains intact, and one previous successful site is retained.
