## Experience

Six interactive exhibit types: quantum circuit to chemistry; gene-editing helix and clinical gates; US electricity-demand scenarios; drug-development gates; population versus UN WPP 2024; fusion physics, conversion, export and annual energy balance. Conceptual illustrations are explicitly non-quantitative. No invented trial attrition rates or universal qubit threshold. Electricity cells and population curves use the data.

Preserves the chapter book and native scrolling, with expandable evidence, keyboard-operable controls, stable per-card years, reduced-motion support and a native modal overview. Dark-book chart colors no longer inherit light-mode colors.

## Accuracy and scoring

Removed hard-coded probabilities and the 50%-of-jobs-lost figure. Hiring now shows the 19% relative employment gap with its actual population and comparator. Bars render from the underlying data. Relationships have explicit types; related developments are not presented as prerequisites.

All 43 lines carry a versioned test or explicit narrative-scenario limitation. Five broad scenarios are unscored. Newly added thresholds are editorial operationalizations; existing numerical probabilities are unchanged and have not been independently calibrated to them. The superhuman-cognition title matches its finite task-suite test. Quantum has a full 12-month verification lag. Electricity uses a declared source hierarchy and leaves missing evidence unresolved. Mass-casualty timing is a deadline; Clock retirement uses its stated 2140–2160 window.

Snapshots now freeze question text, definition and timing; old snapshots remain untouched. Same-day snapshots append. Scoring requires explicit adjudication, rationale, sources and a pre-resolution vintage with the same definition. Late outcomes do not automatically become successes. Legacy unversioned claims and narrative scenarios are excluded. Validation rejects changes to frozen tests without a new version.

## Evidence scope

The companion JSON inventories all 43 lines, citation coverage and missing metadata. Nine long-range lines still lack claim-specific citations, visibly disclosed. Metadata coverage is not source verification.

Primary-source checks for the exhibits:

- Stanford / ADP: 19% relative employment gap for young workers in AI-exposed occupations; not the share of jobs destroyed or proof of the US-and-Europe forecast.
- IBM / UChicago: 70-logical-qubit circuit demonstration; not demonstrated chemistry advantage. Removed the unsupported 1,000-qubit threshold.
- FDA: ex-vivo Casgevy approval establishes a clinical precedent, not in-vivo common-disease approval.
- Intellia: sponsor-reported HAE attack reduction versus placebo is indication-specific, not proof of longevity or common-disease benefit.
- DOE / LBNL: estimated 2024 electricity share 4.7%; 2030 scenarios 9.5–15.3%, reference 11.8%. Not a confidence interval.
- UN WPP 2024: baseline peak around 10.3 billion in the mid-2080s, 10.2 billion in 2100. Site’s earlier peak is an explicit contrary scenario.
- LLNL: fuel gain and annual net facility electricity are distinct.
- Insilico: first Phase III patient is trial progress, not successful Phase III results or approval.
- NIST: standards and migration guidance support a real transition problem, not certainty about a federal completion date.

This pass does not independently re-verify every citation in the registry, establish causality from observational studies, or construct a calibrated probability model. Source URLs are retained in the event histories and citation registry.
